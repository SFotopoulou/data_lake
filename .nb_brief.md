## The demo lake (already implemented: data_lake/demo.py — read it first)
`ensure_demo_lake()` builds (once, ~10 s) a deployment at `$DATA_LAKE_DEMO_DIR` or
`./_demo_lake` with `lake_config.toml`, `data/` (the lake) and `raw/` (the synthetic FITS
inputs: `raw/DEMO_PHOT.fits`, `raw/DEMO_SPEC.fits`, `raw/DEMO_IR.fits`, `raw/spectra/*.fits`,
`raw/cutouts/*.fits`). Ingest token: `DEMO_INGEST_TOKEN` ("demo"). Sky patch: 2°×2° around
RA=150, Dec=2.2, HEALPix order 5 (a handful of tiles).
- `DEMO_PHOT` catalog: `OBJECT_ID` (link id → `_source_id`), `RA`, `DEC`, `CLASS`
  (GALAXY/STAR/QSO), `MAG_G/Z/Y/J/H` + `MAGERR_*` (AB). Cutouts for 150 brightest:
  3 bands (g, z, h), 32×32, nJy, TAN WCS.
- `DEMO_SPEC` catalog: `TARGETID`, `TARGET_RA`, `TARGET_DEC`, `Z`, `ZERR`, `SPECTYPE`;
  300 spectra (generic 1-D, 3600–9800 Å, 4 Å step), linked via `_spectrum_index`.
- `DEMO_IR` catalog: `SOURCE_ID`, `RA`, `DEC`, `W1MPRO`, `W1SIGMPRO`, `W2MPRO`, `W2SIGMPRO` (Vega).
- Lake-local `phot_ab_v1` recipes for DEMO_PHOT (→ phot_ab_g/z/y/j/h) and DEMO_IR (→ phot_ab_w1/w2).
- Verified working on it: `dl-crossmatch DEMO_PHOT DEMO_SPEC --radius-arcsec 1.5`,
  `dl-gather --base DEMO_PHOT --columns '{...}' --radii '{"DEMO_SPEC":1.5,"DEMO_IR":1.5}'
  --cone 150 2.2 --radius-arcsec 3600 --materialize-as demo_joined`,
  `dl-homogenize --from-product demo_joined --transform phot_ab_v1 --materialize-as demo_ab`.
Colours depend on redshift, so a toy photo-z regression works. Values are illustrative.
